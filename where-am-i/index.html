WHERE AM I?
Complete Rules
🎯 Objective
Discover the secret place asking Yes/No questions in English. First team to 3 points wins.
📐 Levels
  • Easy — 30 cards. For beginners.
  • Medium — 50 cards (Easy + Medium).
  • Hard — 60 cards (all levels).
🎙️ Guided Challenge
  1. Teacher draws a card — teams cannot see it.
  2. Teams alternate asking Yes/No questions.
  3. Allowed answers: Yes · No · Usually · Sometimes · Rarely
  4. 1st wrong guess = still in. 2nd wrong guess before 10 questions = round lost.
  5. After 10 questions, guesses are free — no elimination.
⚡ Speed Round
  1. Set a timer: 3–5 minutes.
  2. One team plays, other waits.
  3. Correct guess = 1 point + next card immediately.
  4. Most places at the end wins.
💡 Teacher Tips
  • Allow Portuguese discussion but require questions in English with correct grammar.
  • Write clues on the board: Is it big? → Yes
  • Hard cards: let vocabulary emerge naturally. When a student says "I think it's estufa but I don't know in English!" — that's your moment: "Yes! It's a Greenhouse!"
